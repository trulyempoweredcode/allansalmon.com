About me

Am I the right counsellor for you?

I wonder if you are noticing any of the following in your life: feeling anxious or overwhelmed, people-pleasing or masking, a history of feeling dismissed or invalidated, difficulties with relationships, trust or boundaries, or experiences of abuse.

Being truly heard and seen can be a rare and powerful experience, especially if the relationships in your life have been difficult or complicated. Counselling can be a space for you to make sense of your past, to honour the present, and to move towards a future that's right for you.

I run my own private practice in Fareham, offering sessions both in person and online. I also work as a counsellor for Paragon/The You Trust, a charity supporting those affected by trauma, domestic abuse, sexual abuse and addiction.

How I can help

Support tailored to you

I support my clients to

  • Explore unhelpful patterns and feelings of being stuck
  • Re-connect with your physical body and emotions
  • Reduce feelings of anxiety, depression, shame and pain
  • Increase clarity, empowerment, self-esteem and understanding
  • Improve relationships with yourself and others

Ways we might work together

Creative tools for your sessions

Sometimes words aren't quite enough, and it can help to explore things in a different way. These are some of the creative tools we might use together when talking doesn't feel like the right fit.

Archetype Cards

Explore the roles and patterns that shape how you relate to yourself and others.

Therapy Cards

Beautifully illustrated cards that help name feelings and experiences that are hard to put into words.

Values Cards

Discover what matters most to you: a hands-on way to explore your core values.

Journey Cards

Visual prompts that invite reflection on where you are and where you'd like to be.

Sand Tray Therapy

A powerful, wordless way to explore feelings and experiences using miniature figures and sand.

Sandstory Scenes

Building scenes in the sand tray can reveal patterns and feelings that are difficult to express with words alone.

Getting started

What to expect

Please note, I am currently fully booked at the moment, but if you would like to work with me in future please feel free to drop me a message to arrange an introductory meeting (online or in person). During our meeting we can discuss our availability and waiting list times. Thank you, Allan.

1

Free Chemistry Call

A free 20-minute call, by phone or Google Meet, where you can get a sense of how it feels to be in conversation with me, ask questions, and share a little about why you'd like to come to counselling.

2

First Session

If after the call you'd like to book, we'll arrange a first session. This allows us to talk about the finer points of counselling and explore ways we can make it work for you.

3

Ongoing Support

Sessions are available weekly or bi-weekly, in person at my Fareham practice or online via Google Meet. We work at your pace, for as long as feels right.
