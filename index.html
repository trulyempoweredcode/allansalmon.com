About me

Am I the right counsellor for you?

I wonder if you are noticing any of the following in your life:

  • Feeling anxious or overwhelmed
  • People-pleasing or masking
  • A history of feeling dismissed or invalidated
  • Difficulties with relationships, trust or boundaries
  • Experiences of abuse

I run my own private practice in Fareham, offering sessions both in person and online. I also work as a counsellor for Paragon/The You Trust, a charity supporting those affected by trauma, domestic abuse, sexual abuse and addiction.

How I can help

Support tailored to you

I support my clients to

  • Explore unhelpful patterns and feelings of being stuck
  • Re-connect with your physical body and emotions
  • Reduce feelings of anxiety, depression, shame and pain
  • Increase clarity, empowerment, self-esteem and understanding
  • Improve relationships with yourself and others

Therapeutic approaches

  • Integrative Counselling
  • Person-Centred Therapy
  • Psychodynamic Therapy
  • Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)
  • Somatic Therapy
  • Transactional Analysis
  • Sand Tray Therapy
  • Mindfulness

Areas I work with

  • Anxiety and panic attacks
  • Depression and low mood
  • Trauma and PTSD
  • Abuse and domestic violence
  • Addiction
  • Self-esteem and confidence
  • Relationship difficulties
  • ADHD and neurodiversity
