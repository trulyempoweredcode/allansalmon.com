Am I the right
counsellor for you?

I wonder if you are noticing any of the following in your life: Feeling anxious and overwhelmed? Saying "yes", when really you mean "no"? A history of feeling dismissed or invalidated? Putting others' needs before your own? Feeling the impact from traumatic relationships or experiences of abuse.

Being truly heard and seen can be a rare and powerful experience, especially if the relationships in your life have been difficult or complicated. Counselling can be a space for you to make sense of your past, to honour the present, and to move towards a future that's right for you.

I run a private practice in Fareham, offering sessions both in person and online. I have previously worked as a counsellor for Paragon/The You Trust, a charity supporting those affected by trauma, abuse and addiction.

Support tailored to you

I support my clients to

  • Explore patterns and feelings of being stuck
  • Re-connect with the physical body and emotions
  • Reduce feelings of anxiety, depression, shame 
  • Increased understanding and compassion for self
  • Improve relationships and install healthy boundaries

Ways we might work together

Creative tools for your sessions

Sometimes words aren't quite enough and  it can help to explore things in a different way. These are just some of the creative tools/methods we might use in our sessions together

Archetype Cards

Explore roles and patterns that may have shaped how you relate to yourself, others and the world.

Therapy Cards

Beautifully illustrated cards that help name feelings and experiences that are hard to put into words.

Values Cards

Discover, organise and honour what matters most to you in your life

Journey Cards

Visual prompts that invite reflection on where you are, or where you'd like to be.

Sand Tray

A powerful, creative way to explore feelings and experiences

Sandstory Scenes

Telling your story in the sand can reveal patterns and emotions that may be difficult to express with words alone.

The space

Your counselling room

Feeling safe and comfortable is different for everyone, and the space should reflect what that means for you. In the counselling room you'll find low-level lighting, fidget toys, blankets, sound bowls, soft cushions, a bluetooth speaker, pens and doodle books. You can sit on the floor, stand or have your feet up. My aim is to make the space work for you.

Getting started

What to expect

01

Free "Chemistry Call"

A free 20 min meeting (either in person or via Google Meet) where you can get a sense of how it feels to be in conversation with me, ask questions, and share a little of what you might like to bring to counselling. It's a good chance for us to see how we might work together.

02

First Session

If after the meeting you'd like to book, we'd arrange a time/day to have regular sessions. In our first session (which is half price) we'd discuss some of the finer points of counselling, including; confidentiality and our therapeutic agreement, any goals you may have and a brief history. We'll also discuss how we tailor our sessions so that they work for you. 

03

Ongoing Support

Our sessions continue at the same time/day each week until such a time that feels right to you to end. Periodically I will invite reviews of our work together to help keep things moving in the right direction.
