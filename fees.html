Getting started

Fees and what to expect

One simple fee, a free call first, and sessions at your pace.

What to expect

Three gentle steps to begin

1

Free chemistry call

A free 20-minute call, by phone or Google Meet, where you can get a sense of how it feels to be in conversation with me, ask questions, and share a little about why you'd like to come to counselling.

2

First session

If after the call you'd like to book, we'll arrange a first session. This allows us to talk about the finer points of counselling and explore ways we can make it work for you.

3

Ongoing support

Sessions are available weekly or bi-weekly, in person at my Fareham practice or online via Google Meet, Zoom or WhatsApp. We work at your pace, for as long as feels right.

I am currently fully booked. If you would like to work with me in future, please feel free to drop me a message to arrange an introductory meeting (online or in person). During our meeting we can discuss our availability and waiting list times. Thank you, Allan.
