Privacy Policy

How I collect, use and keep your information safe

Any personal data provided by you to Allan Salmon Counselling (through any means: verbal, written, electronic, or via my website) will be held and processed in accordance with the principles of the Data Protection Act 1998 and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Your data will only be used for the purposes for which you have given consent: to provide the counselling services you have requested from me, and to meet my legitimate interests as a counsellor.

Introduction

I, Allan Salmon, am the data controller for the personal information you share with me. I am committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how I collect, use, and store your information. It only applies to data collected directly by me, whether through my forms, website, or in person. Third-party websites or services linked to my site are not covered by this policy.

If you have any questions about your personal information or how it is used, please feel free to contact me.

When you request counselling with me, or otherwise provide your personal details, you will be asked to consent to my processing of your data under the terms of this policy.

What information do I collect?

Counselling requests

When you request counselling (verbally, in writing, or electronically), I may collect your name, address, telephone number(s), and email address. I may also ask for details such as your availability, therapeutic needs, and other relevant information.

Initial appointments

At our first meeting, I may ask about your current personal, social, medical, and financial circumstances. I may also ask about your background and family history, as well as the issues that are bringing you to counselling. This helps me decide whether I can offer you counselling and how best to support you.

Website

My website may use Google Analytics to collect anonymous data about user behaviour and web traffic. Google's own privacy policies govern their use of this data.

Other forms

If you complete any enquiry or application forms, these may include your name, postal address, email address, phone number, and any messages you choose to share with me.

How do I use your information?

I use information held about you in the following ways:

  • To provide you with professional counselling services.
  • To offer suitable counselling appointments and manage scheduling.
  • To notify you of any changes to appointments or services.
  • To seek feedback from you about your experience of counselling.
  • To improve the service I provide.
  • To administer my counselling practice (e.g. appointment records, invoices, payments).
  • To stay in touch with those who consent, for the purposes of professional practice and development.

When might I share your information?

I will not share your personal information with others, except in the following situations:

With your consent

If you ask me to share information with another professional (e.g. GP, psychiatrist), or agree that I may do so.

To prevent serious harm

If I believe disclosure is necessary to prevent serious harm to you or someone else.

Legal compliance

If required to do so by law, regulation, or professional obligation.

How do I keep your information safe?

I take the security of your information seriously.

  • Paper notes and correspondence are kept securely in locked storage.
  • Electronic records are kept on password-protected devices or with reputable, secure cloud-based providers.
  • While I take steps to protect information sent electronically, please note that transmission via the internet (including email) can never be completely secure.
  • Client notes and documentation are retained for a minimum of 6 years, or indefinitely where appropriate, unless you request otherwise.
  • Electronic payments are handled by third-party providers, and their own privacy policies apply in those instances.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the information I hold about you.
  • Ask me to correct any inaccuracies in your information.
  • Withdraw your consent for me to hold or process your data at any time. Please note: if you withdraw consent while actively receiving counselling, I would no longer be able to provide the service.

If you wish to exercise these rights, please contact me directly at hello@allansalmon.com.
